Rebuilding the full panel

With Python 3.10 or later and the packages of requirements.txt:

# public downloads, no account needed
python3 closed/build_french.py
python3 closed/build_epu.py
python3 closed/build_gpr.py
python3 closed/build_fred_thirdparty.py
# licensed: your WRDS subscriptions (CRSP; Compustat and I/B/E/S for earnings)
WRDS_USER=your_username python3 closed/build_crsp.py
WRDS_USER=your_username python3 closed/build_earnings.py
python3 closed/verify.py          # which blocks and columns match our vintage
python3 build_full.py             # open panel + rebuilt blocks -> full daily panel
python3 closed/build_monthly.py   # licensed monthly blocks
python3 closed/verify.py --monthly
python3 build_full.py --monthly   # full monthly panel

Credentials are read from environment variables only and are never written to disk. Without WRDS access, python3 build_full.py --allow-missing builds the panel with the CRSP and earnings columns left empty; the Ken French market return ff_mkt is a close substitute for mkt_ret.

What to expect

Public sources are revised and extended over time, so a fresh download can differ from our vintage. closed/verify.py reports, for each block and column, whether your rebuild is byte-identical to ours, and compares the coverage. Each rebuilt column stays under its provider's terms.
