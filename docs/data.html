Reading the data

Each row of the daily panel is one NYSE session, keyed by trading_date. The return of session t runs from the close of session t−1 to the close of session t, so an event belongs to the first session whose close comes at or after it. Columns tagged ex_ante in the codebook are known before the session opens; columns tagged realized become known during or after the session and should be lagged by one session in forecasts. The documentation gives the dating rules, the quality grades and the licenses.

import pandas as pd
daily = pd.read_parquet("trading_days_daily_open.parquet")
codebook = pd.read_csv("codebook_daily.csv")
ex_ante = codebook.loc[codebook.timing == "ex_ante", "column"]

Monthly version

The monthly panel aggregates the daily data by calendar month: event flags become counts and shares of the month's sessions, continuous series become monthly means, extremes and month-end values, daily returns are compounded, and the calendar gives the number of sessions, weekdays, closures and early closes. Each monthly column is tagged known_at_start if its value is fixed before the month begins, as for the number of scheduled FOMC meetings, and realized_in_month otherwise.

Licensed columns

52 daily columns come from licensed or third-party sources and are not in these files. Scripts rebuild them with your own access and merge them with the open panel into the full panel.
