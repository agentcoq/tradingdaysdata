Characteristics of every NYSE trading day since 1960

This website hosts 356 characteristics of each of the 16,610 NYSE sessions from 1960 to 2025, all built from publicly available sources: the calendar, scheduled macroeconomic and monetary-policy announcements, political, sports and natural events, weather and astronomy, and attention. Every event is placed on the first session in which the market could react to it, and every column records where its dates come from. The 52 other characteristics of the paper, mostly market conditions and earnings announcements, come from licensed sources and cannot be redistributed; our scripts rebuild them from your own access.

What is in the dataset

The 356 columns are released under CC BY 4.0 or, for the columns derived from Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0. The 52 licensed characteristics come from CRSP, Compustat, I/B/E/S and a few third-party series; the rebuild scripts check your result against our checksums. The codebook lists all 408 columns and gives, for each, its source, the first date with data, a quality grade, whether its value is known before the open, and its license. A monthly version aggregates the daily data into 792 months.

Updates

The data are updated once a year, in the first quarter, when the year-end data are available. Each update is a new version with its own DOI; earlier versions stay available unchanged.
